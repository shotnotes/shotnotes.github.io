Data privacy

Your personal data belongs to you. Shot Notes is intentionally engineered across all platforms to ensure absolute user privacy: it does not collect, store, track, or share any personal information, device identifiers, or behavioural data. All of your data is saved strictly on your individual local device(s). Any features you choose to use, such as manually exporting data to an external file system, generating QR codes to share kit information, or opting in to look up a location name using Apple or Google APIs, are completely optional and run entirely under your control. There are no background analytics or third-party ad trackers, and any premium subscription billing is handled securely and anonymously by the official app stores.

For complete technical transparency regarding on-device local error logging or the open-source GitHub bug reporting system, please read the full Privacy Policy.
