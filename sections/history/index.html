A brief history

Long before digital displays offered instant feedback, photographers relied on physical notebooks to anchor their intentions and bridge the gap between capturing the subject on film and reviewing the final frame. Using automatic digital cameras made keeping shot notes feel like a tedious distraction – an awkward friction added to a fast-paced workflow. Embracing a manual lens, especially on a Leica M, changes that: the tactile, deliberate rhythm of manual shooting naturally extends into documenting the moment. The true essence of these notes is not recording metadata, but cultivating a reflective space to revisit one’s choices, build meta-cognition around one’s vision, and intentionally refine one’s craft.

Keeping a physical notebook and pen in our pockets is no longer common, but our smartphones are always with us. That makes Shot Notes a seamless bridge between modern convenience and mindful photography.
