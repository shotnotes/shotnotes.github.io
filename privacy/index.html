Privacy policy

Effective Date: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the software application Shot Notes (including all mobile and desktop versions), collectively referred to as the “Application”, created by Anirban Basu (the “Developer”). The mobile versions of the Application are provided as a free-to-download utility with optional in-app subscriptions, while the desktop counterparts are completely free to download and require no subscriptions. Across all supported devices and platforms, the Application is intended for use as is.

Absolute Commitment to Privacy

Your personal data belongs to you. The Application is intentionally engineered across all platforms to protect user privacy.

The Application does not collect, store, transmit, process, or share personal information, unique device identifiers, or derivative behavioural information of any kind. Any financial transactions or subscription management on mobile platforms are processed entirely by the respective storefront platform (Apple App Store or Google Play Store); the Developer does not receive, access, or store your billing details, names, or payment information.

Marketing and Behavioural Tracking

  • No Analytics: There are no third-party analytics tools, software development kits (SDKs), or crash-reporting frameworks that track, compile, or compare user behaviour across either mobile or desktop platforms.

  • No Advertising: The Application does not contain advertising networks and will not target you with marketing messages.

Core Functionality and On-Device Storage

To function properly, the Application may save basic, non-identifiable settings or configuration files strictly within your device’s secure local storage.

  • Essential Configuration: This local storage is utilized solely for essential application execution, falling under the “strictly necessary” exemptions of global privacy directives. This data is never sent over the internet or shared with the Developer.

  • On-Device Error Logging: The Application keeps a short log of its own technical errors entirely on your local device. This log captures the time, the Application’s version, the type of error, its stack trace, and how many times it has occurred. For errors originating from the Flutter framework, it also tracks the first line of the error message describing widgets and layout. This log is specifically engineered to never hold your captured shots, notes, places, or GPS coordinates. This local error log is never transmitted anywhere unless you explicitly choose to share it, and you can manually clear this log at any time from the settings screen.

Non-mandatory, User-initiated Features

The Application contains features that allow you to interact with data or external systems. These features are entirely optional, are not required for the core functioning of the Application, and operate with the same high privacy standards across mobile and desktop environments:

  • Optional Export to Cloud Storage or External File System: You may choose to manually export your data to external third-party services of your choice (such as Dropbox or a networked file system). These transfers are handled entirely by your operating system’s native file-sharing systems. The Developer has no access to your cloud credentials or exported files.

  • On-Device QR Code Export: The Application allows you to manually export individual records of your kit items as a QR code. This generation happens entirely locally on your device. No data is transmitted to an external server or processed by any third party to generate this code.

  • Opt-In Location Lookup (GPS Data) and Map Lookup: If you choose to use the location name lookup feature, the mobile versions of the Application will request access to your device’s GPS location. This is entirely optional. If you grant permission, your GPS coordinates are sent directly and respectively to Apple (on iOS) or Google (on Android) to translate the coordinates into a location name via their cloud-hosted APIs. Similarly, you may choose to look up your location on the map by sending your GPS or typed location to your default map application but this lookup is optional. Apple and Google handle the GPS coordinates under their own policies: Apple’s privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy. The Developer does not receive, view, or log this location data.

  • User-Initiated Troubleshooting & Support (GitHub Reporting): Navigating to Settings > Report a problem allows you to voluntarily open a support form via your external web browser on GitHub. This action pre-fills a form with your Application version, operating system, and device model. The interface explicitly displays what the report will contain, allowing you to choose whether your local error log is appended to it. Please be aware that submitting this form creates a public issue in a Shot Notes repository that anyone can read. Any personal information or GitHub account data disclosed during this interaction falls entirely under GitHub’s General Privacy Statement.

Global Regulatory Compliance (GDPR, CCPA, COPPA)

The Application is designed to inherently comply with strict global privacy frameworks, including the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Because the Application collects and retains absolutely zero personal data:

  • Your data rights under these laws—including the right to access, delete, restrict processing, or opt-out of data sales—are fundamentally satisfied, as no data exists to be processed, accessed, or deleted.

  • The Application is entirely safe for users of all ages, as no information is gathered from or targeted at children or minors.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect minor functional updates. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any changes are effective immediately upon publishing the updated text.
