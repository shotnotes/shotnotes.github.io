A shared kit item

Someone has shared a piece of their kit with you, such as a lens or a filter, as a link or a QR code. Shot Notes is the app that opens it.

If you have Shot Notes

Open the link again on your phone, or scan the code with the Camera app. Shot Notes shows what was shared, and adds only what you tick. You can also copy the link and paste it under Add from a link, at the top of the Kit tab. The support pages say how sharing works.

If you do not have it yet

Shot Notes is a quiet log for photographers’ notes on shots taken with manual lenses, kept on your own devices. It is in beta, open to invited iOS testers, with Android coming soon: Get started says how things stand. When you have it, open the link again.

What was shared

The link holds only the details of the item: its kind, maker and name, and a few fields such as the focal lengths. It never holds the sender’s shots, places or notes, and it holds a serial number only if the sender chose to send it. These details are in the part of the link after the #. Your browser does not send that part to this website, and this page does not read it, so the website never sees what was shared. The privacy policy tells the rest.
